# Social Media Accessibility

Applies to every post, graphic, story, and platform video across LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and anywhere else Envision publishes. Social is where Envision's own community is most likely to meet the brand, so these are non-negotiable, not nice-to-haves.

## Alt text on every image

- Use the platform's alt text field on every image, every time, composed when the post is composed. LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram all support it.
- Describe content and function, front-loading the key information. Skip "image of" or "photo of"; screen readers already announce the element type. Include names of pictured people when known and relevant, and the pillar-correct framing: the person and what they are doing, agency first.
- If the graphic contains text, that text appears in full in the alt text or the post copy. Never let words exist only as pixels.

## The message never lives only in the graphic

Whatever the graphic says, the post copy says too. Stat cards, event details, quotes: repeat them as live text in the caption. This serves screen reader users, anyone on low bandwidth, and search, all at once.

## Hashtags and copy mechanics

- **CamelCase every multi-word hashtag**: #WhiteCaneDayWalk, #EnvisionCareers, #SupplierSummit. Screen readers pronounce the words; lowercase runs become noise.
- Hashtags grouped at the end of the post, not woven mid-sentence.
- Emoji sparingly: never as bullets, never in repeated runs, never replacing a word mid-sentence. Place them at the end of a thought so they punctuate rather than interrupt.
- No Unicode pseudo-fonts (the bold, script, or outline character generators). Screen readers skip or spell them out letter by letter, and they break search.
- Links descriptive where the platform allows link text. Where it does not, say plainly where the link goes: "Registration link in our bio."

## Graphics

- Contrast per the hub tables. On the common white-ground social layouts: Charcoal or Navy text, Green and Goldenrod as bars and accents only, never as the words.
- Type sized for the feed, not the design canvas: on a 1080px canvas, treat roughly 40px as the floor for anything that must be read, larger for the headline. If it is not legible at thumbnail size, it is decoration.
- No text over busy photography without a solid or scrim backing.
- Meaning never by color alone in any infographic element.

## Stories, reels, and platform video

- Captions on every video, always: platform or burned-in, and auto-captions are a draft, never the ship version. Review for accuracy, punctuation, and speaker identification.
- Text stays inside the platform safe zones and on screen long enough to be read aloud twice.
- Sound-off comprehension is the bar for feed video; the full production rules live in `references/video.md`.
