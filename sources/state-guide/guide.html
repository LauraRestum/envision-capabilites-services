Ordering supplies for your post

A quick reference for Desk Officers and overseas posts: find required supplies, then choose a delivery route before you order.

Share this guide with your post’s procurement and shipping teams.

The mandatory source requirement, in plain language

For supplies, follow the RFO FAR 8.103 source priorities: agency inventory, other agencies’ excess, Federal Prison Industries, then AbilityOne Procurement List items. When a requirement is covered by the Procurement List, buy through the required AbilityOne source unless an authorized purchase exception applies. A government purchase card or small purchase does not bypass this requirement. Commercial products that are essentially the same as a listed item are also covered.

Authority: RFO FAR 8.103 and 8.202

Already working with another provider?

Envision is one compliant source for AbilityOne products. If your post already buys from another Base Supply Center or authorized AbilityOne source, keep using it. If you don’t have one, we’re here to help.

How to order: find it, then ship it

1Find it

Shop your Department of State approved catalog on EnvisionXpress.com. Search by NSN, part number, or keyword, or filter to the AbilityOne assortment. The compliance check is already built in: products labeled AbilityOne are Procurement List items, and everything else in the catalog is a commercial product that is not essentially the same as one. Put what you need in one cart and order with confidence.

Shop EnvisionXpress.com|Browse AbilityOne products|Don’t see what you need? Request a quote and our team will help you find it.

2Choose your delivery route

To post

  • For APO/FPO, look for the Ships APO badge. If an item doesn’t have it, contact customer support to have it added or to find an approved replacement.
  • Enter PSC, Unit, or Box as the street address. Choose AA, AE, or AP and use the assigned ZIP.
  • For DPO or direct international delivery, ask Envision to confirm eligibility and routing before ordering.

To a State despatch facility

  • Ask your post’s shipping team for the approved U.S. despatch address, post identifier, marking instructions, and any receiving requirements.
  • Share these with Envision before ordering so the team can confirm the shipment to that facility.
  • Coordinate onward movement with State.

Carriers: FedEx International (preferred, typically about one week, with enhanced tracking) or USPS (typically two to three weeks), both at negotiated GSA rates. Confirm restrictions and timing for your route: APO/FPO shipping FAQ|Shipping policy

Two ways to order

Order online with pricing up front

Best for routine and single-item purchases. Select from your Department of State approved catalog on EnvisionXpress.com. Shipping is calculated at checkout, so you see the total delivered cost before you order.

Work with our customer service team

Best for larger, recurring, or special orders. Request a quote and we’ll consolidate quantities, apply volume pricing, coordinate shipments, and set up scheduled or recurring deliveries. Include your post name, items or NSNs, quantities, needed-by date, and delivery route.

For larger or regional needs, our team can look at options like consolidating orders and shipping to a central location for distribution, to find the most practical price for where you need it.

What about cost?

  • Online orders show the total delivered cost, shipping included, before you buy.
  • For larger or recurring orders, a quote can combine quantities and apply volume pricing.
  • Shipping uses negotiated GSA rates.
