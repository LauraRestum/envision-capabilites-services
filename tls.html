SECTION 02 // THE VEHICLE 02 / 06

A pre-set ceiling. No re-bidding.

IDIQ stands for Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity — the federal contract type that lets agencies order from a pre-set ceiling without running a new competition for every purchase. The ABOBSC TLS contract puts $150 million of that capacity behind Envision-made product.

// 01 · The Contract

$150M, 5-year IDIQ

Envision is the prime vendor on the AbilityOne Base Supply Center Tailored Logistics Support contract, administered by DLA Troop Support. Ordering runs against a pre-approved ceiling, so product moves at the speed of the requirement — not the speed of a bid cycle.

// 02 · The Pricing

Reviewed and approved

All pricing on the vehicle is considered fair and reasonable by DLA Troop Support, and Base Supply Center purchases carry pre-approved AbilityOne pricing. GPC and IMPAC cards are accepted.

// 03 · The Capacity

$198M combined with H2F

TLS is one of two active 5-year DLA prime vendor contracts Envision holds — $150M on ABOBSC TLS and $48M on the Holistic Health & Fitness (H2F) program — for $198 million in combined contract capacity. Each ships Envision SKUs.

SECTION 04 // THE NETWORK 04 / 06

The retail face of the channel.

Envision's Base Supply Centers are where the TLS vehicle meets the customer — 16 Envision-operated locations across 10 states, part of the AbilityOne BSC network spanning 160+ bases in 14 countries.

Stores stock office supplies, janitorial, safety apparel, and SKILCRAFT product lines for active-duty personnel and federal buyers, with Envision-manufactured products flowing through the channel from our U.S. campuses in Wichita, Kansas and Dallas, Texas.

Pre-approved AbilityOne pricing means no competitive bid cycle is required, and GPC and IMPAC cards are accepted at the counter.
