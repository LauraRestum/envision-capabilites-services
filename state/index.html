Why this matters

The mandatory source requirement, in plain language

For supplies, follow the RFO FAR 8.103 source priorities: agency inventory, other agencies’ excess, Federal Prison Industries, then AbilityOne Procurement List items. When a requirement is covered by the Procurement List, buy through the required AbilityOne source unless an authorized purchase exception applies. A government purchase card or small purchase does not bypass this requirement. Commercial products that are essentially the same as a listed item are also covered.

Authority: RFO FAR 8.103 and 8.202

Already working with another provider?

Envision is one compliant source for AbilityOne products. If your post already buys from another Base Supply Center or authorized AbilityOne source, keep using it. If you don’t have one, we’re here to help.

How to order

How to order: find it, then ship it

Find what your post needs, then choose a delivery route before you check out.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Find it

    Shop your Department of State approved catalog on EnvisionXpress.com. Search by NSN, part number, or keyword, or filter to the AbilityOne assortment. The compliance check is already built in: products labeled AbilityOne are Procurement List items, and everything else in the catalog is a commercial product that is not essentially the same as one. Put what you need in one cart and order with confidence.

    Shop EnvisionXpress.com Browse AbilityOne products

    Don’t see what you need? Request a quote and our team will help you find it.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Choose your delivery route

    Confirm product restrictions, freight charges, and timing for your route.

    To post

    • For APO/FPO, look for the Ships APO badge. If an item doesn’t have it, contact customer support to have the item added or to find an approved replacement.
    • Enter PSC, Unit, or Box as the street address. Choose AA, AE, or AP and use the assigned ZIP.
    • For DPO or direct international delivery, ask Envision to confirm eligibility and routing before ordering.

    To a State despatch facility

    • Ask your post’s shipping team for the approved U.S. despatch address, post identifier, marking instructions, and any receiving requirements.
    • Share these with Envision before ordering so the team can confirm the shipment to that facility.
    • Coordinate onward movement with State.
